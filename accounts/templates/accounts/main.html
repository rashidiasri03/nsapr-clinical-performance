CLINICAL PERFORMANCE SYSTEM
(CPS)

ABOUT US

The proposed NSAPR framework aims to systematically evaluate the surgical and anaesthesia performance, to identify gaps and propose improvement measures across all healthcare levels.

Team Clinical Performance

Vision

To be a paragon of healthcare performance in MOH Malaysia, where each clinical and non-clinical area contributes to improved health outcomes and a sustainable healthcare system.

Mission

To ensure strategic oversight of clinical performance by evaluating service standards, patient outcomes, resource balance, and enabling data-driven decisions for continuous improvement and equitable care.

Objectives

  • Benchmark surgical and anaesthesia services nationally and internationally.
  • Integrate improvement measures into MOH policy and planning.
  • Identify training needs for continuous professional development.
  • Enhance accountability through structured reporting and audit processes.
  • Promote evidence-based practices and adherence to clinical guidelines.
  • Strengthen safety in surgical and anaesthesia care.
